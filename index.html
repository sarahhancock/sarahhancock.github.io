About Me

I am a staff researcher in Daniel Jacob's Atmospheric Chemistry Modeling Group at Harvard University, based in Santa Cruz, California. I received my Ph.D. in Environmental Science and Engineering from Harvard in May 2026. My research focuses on methane emissions, with an emphasis on using atmospheric observations and modeling to better understand where emissions come from and how they vary across regions and sectors. Outside of research, I enjoy vegan baking, swimming, and spending time in the sun.
